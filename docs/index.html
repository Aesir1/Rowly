Free & Open Source

ROWLY

· Sync by Nature ·

An open-source rowing companion for Android that tracks your strokes, speed and distance — on the water or on the ergometer — and guides you through structured trainings with voice announcements, so your eyes stay on the water. The complete source code is public: inspect it, build it yourself, or contribute.

Source code on GitHub Support the project
Live metricsStroke rate, speed, distance and time, readable from the far end of a boat.
TrainingsPresets like Pyramid, Tabata and Half Marathon, or build your own phases.
Voice guidancePhase changes announced out loud — no need to look at the screen.
Ergometer modeIndoor sessions without GPS, recorded alongside your outdoor activities.
